# Problem Statement

Small and rural entrepreneurs in Kerala often struggle to identify the right business opportunities for their specific location, skills, available capital, and risk capacity.

Existing business advisory tools generally provide generic recommendations and do not sufficiently consider local competition, demand indicators, seasonal factors, financial requirements, or the entrepreneur's individual circumstances.

As a result, aspiring entrepreneurs may make business decisions based on assumptions or incomplete information, increasing the risk of financial loss and business failure.

GramGrow AI aims to address this problem by providing localized, data-driven business recommendations and financial guidance for rural entrepreneurs in Kerala.
