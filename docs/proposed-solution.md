# Proposed Solution

GramGrow AI is an AI-powered business advisory system designed for rural entrepreneurs in Kerala.

The system combines the entrepreneur's skills, experience, available capital, business interests, and risk preference with local economic and business data.

It analyzes these factors to:

- Identify suitable business opportunities.
- Rank multiple business options based on the entrepreneur's profile and local conditions.
- Analyze local competition and demand indicators.
- Estimate startup costs, monthly expenses, revenue, break-even point, and working capital requirements.
- Assess business risks and explain the reasons behind the risk level.
- Allow users to test different what-if scenarios.
- Calculate the funding gap and suggest relevant financing options.
- Provide a step-by-step action plan before starting the business.
- Monitor actual business performance after launch and compare it with the original estimates.

The system is designed to provide transparent, localized, and data-driven guidance rather than generic business recommendations.
